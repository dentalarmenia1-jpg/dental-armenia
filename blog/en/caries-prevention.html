Cavities: How They Form and How to Prevent Them

A cavity (caries) is the gradual breakdown of hard tooth tissue, linked to bacteria and sugars.

How it forms

Bacteria in the mouth turn sugars into acids, which gradually weaken tooth enamel.

How to prevent it

  • Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste.
  • Clean between your teeth.
  • Limit sweets and sugary drinks, especially frequent snacking.
  • Have preventive check-ups with a dentist at the frequency your doctor recommends.

When to see a doctor

If you notice dark spots, holes, or pain from sweet or cold foods, it is better to get checked. In the early stage, the problem is usually easier to treat.
