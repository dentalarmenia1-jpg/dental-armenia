Baby Teeth: Why They Need Care

Baby teeth are temporary, but their health matters for your child.

Why they matter

  • They help with chewing and speaking.
  • They hold space for permanent teeth.
  • Early loss can affect the position of permanent teeth.
  • Untreated cavities can cause pain and infection.

How to care for them

  • Start cleaning as soon as the first tooth appears.
  • Use an age-appropriate brush and toothpaste; ask your doctor about the amount of fluoride.
  • An adult should help or supervise young children while brushing.
  • Limit sugary drinks and frequent snacks.
  • Do not let your child fall asleep with a bottle of a sweet drink.

When to see a doctor

If you notice dark spots on the teeth, your child complains of pain or a tooth is damaged, see a pediatric dentist.
