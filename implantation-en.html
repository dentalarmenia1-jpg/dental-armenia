A few words about implants

A dental implant is an artificial root placed in the jawbone that supports a crown, bridge or denture. Implants are considered when one, several or all teeth are missing. Whether implantation is possible, the method and the timeline are determined by a doctor after an examination and any necessary tests.

Frequently asked questions

What is a dental implant?

An artificial root placed in the jawbone that serves as support for a crown, bridge or denture.

Who can apply for implantation?

A doctor decides this based on your health and examinations. Sending a request does not obligate you to anything.

How much does implantation cost?

The cost depends on the specific case, the chosen specialist and the materials used. Details are clarified during the conversation.
